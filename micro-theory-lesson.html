Intermediate Macroeconomics I

1. GDP (Gross Domestic Product)

GDP measures the total value of goods and services produced in an economy.

  • Production Method: Value added at each stage
  • Income Method: Wages + Rent + Profit + Interest
  • Expenditure Method: C + I + G + (X - M)

2. Phillips Curve

Shows inverse relationship between inflation and unemployment.

  • Short run: Trade-off exists
  • Long run: No trade-off (vertical curve)

3. Expectations

Expectations affect inflation and economic decisions.

  • Adaptive: Based on past inflation
  • Rational: Based on all available information

5. Economic Growth
