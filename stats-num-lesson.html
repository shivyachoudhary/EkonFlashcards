Mastering numericals

1. Demand & Supply

Demand slopes downward while supply slopes upward. Their intersection determines equilibrium price and quantity.

2. Phillips Curve

Shows inverse relationship between inflation and unemployment in the short run.

3. IS-LM Model

IS and LM curves intersect to determine equilibrium output and interest rate.

4. Wage vs Leisure

Higher wages increase opportunity cost of leisure, affecting labor supply.

5. AD-AS Model

Aggregate demand and supply determine overall output and price level.
